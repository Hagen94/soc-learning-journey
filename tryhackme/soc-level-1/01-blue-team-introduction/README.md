# Module 01 - Blue Team Introduction

## Overview

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of defensive cybersecurity and the role of the Blue Team in protecting an organization's assets.

Throughout these rooms, I explored the responsibilities of a Junior Security Analyst, the structure of a Security Operations Center (SOC), and the two primary attack vectors that organizations must defend against: humans and systems.

The knowledge gained in this module establishes the foundation for the remaining SOC Level 1 learning path.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this module, I am able to:

- Understand the purpose of a Security Operations Center (SOC).
- Explain the responsibilities of a Junior Security Analyst.
- Identify the role of the Blue Team within an organization.
- Recognize common attack vectors targeting both people and systems.
- Understand how mitigation and detection complement each other in modern cybersecurity.

## Key Takeaways

This module emphasizes that effective cybersecurity requires protecting both **people** and **technology**.

Attackers constantly search for the easiest path into an organization, whether by manipulating employees through social engineering or exploiting weaknesses in systems.

A Security Operations Center plays a critical role by combining **prevention, detection, investigation, and response** to reduce organizational risk.

---

## Module Summary

The four rooms in this module provide the foundation required for the SOC Level 1 learning path.

Together, they explain:

- What a SOC is.
- What a Junior Security Analyst does.
- How Blue Teams operate.
- Why humans are targeted.
- Why systems are targeted.
- How organizations defend against both attack vectors.

This knowledge serves as the starting point for understanding more advanced SOC concepts, security technologies, and incident response processes covered in later modules.
