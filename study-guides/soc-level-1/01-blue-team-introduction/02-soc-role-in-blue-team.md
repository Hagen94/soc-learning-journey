# Study Guide - SOC Role in Blue Team

> Module 01 - Blue Team Introduction

---

# Learning Summary

This room explains how the Blue Team fits into an organization's cybersecurity structure. It introduces the relationship between the Security Operations Center (SOC), other security teams, and the career paths available within defensive cybersecurity.

**Goal:** Understand how a SOC operates within an organization and how different Blue Team roles collaborate to defend against cyber threats.

---

# Key Concepts

## Blue Team

The Blue Team is responsible for defending an organization's infrastructure by monitoring, detecting, investigating, and responding to cyber threats.

### Main Responsibilities

- Security monitoring
- Incident detection
- Incident response
- Threat analysis
- Security improvement

---

## Security Operations Center (SOC)

The SOC is the operational center where security analysts continuously monitor systems, investigate alerts, and coordinate incident response.

Typical responsibilities include:

- Monitoring security alerts
- Investigating suspicious activity
- Escalating incidents
- Coordinating with other teams

---

## Security Organization

Cybersecurity is divided into several specialized teams.

| Team | Primary Responsibility |
|------|-------------------------|
| Blue Team | Defensive security |
| Red Team | Offensive security testing |
| GRC | Governance, Risk, and Compliance |
| CIRT / CSIRT | Incident response |
| SOC | Continuous monitoring and investigation |

---

## Career Progression

A SOC Level 1 position is often the starting point for several cybersecurity careers.

Possible progression:

- SOC L1 Analyst
- SOC L2 Analyst
- SOC Engineer
- Incident Responder
- Threat Hunter
- Digital Forensics Analyst
- Security Manager
- CISO

---

# Important Definitions

## Blue Team

The defensive side of cybersecurity responsible for protecting systems and responding to attacks.

---

## Red Team

Security professionals who simulate attacks to identify weaknesses before real attackers do.

---

## GRC

Governance, Risk, and Compliance.

Focuses on policies, regulations, risk management, and security frameworks.

---

## SOC

A centralized team responsible for security monitoring and incident response.

---

## MSSP

Managed Security Service Provider.

A company that provides security monitoring and SOC services for multiple organizations.

---

## CISO

Chief Information Security Officer.

Executive responsible for an organization's cybersecurity strategy.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Thinking the SOC and the Blue Team are exactly the same.

❌ Believing the Red Team is an "enemy" of the Blue Team.

❌ Assuming cybersecurity is only technical and ignores governance or compliance.

❌ Confusing MSSPs with internal SOCs.

❌ Focusing only on technical skills and ignoring communication and teamwork.
